# References and further reading

Record exact versions, access dates, dataset accession IDs, and filtering steps in each experiment.

## Protein sequence resources

- [UniProt](https://www.uniprot.org/) — protein sequence and functional annotation resource. Check the licence and terms for the particular dataset being used.
- [NCBI Protein](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/protein/) — protein sequence records and accession-based retrieval.

## Generative protein models

- Ferruz, N. et al. (2022). **ProtGPT2 is a deep unsupervised language model for protein design.** *Nature Communications*. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-022-32007-7
- [ESM repository](https://github.com/facebookresearch/esm) — ESM models and tooling. ESM-2 checkpoints are protein language models for representation/prediction tasks; do not assume every ESM checkpoint is an autoregressive sequence generator.
